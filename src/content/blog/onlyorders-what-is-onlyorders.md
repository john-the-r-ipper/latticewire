If you run a small store, most of your day isn't checkout lines — it's messages. A customer asks about a size, comments on a post, or DMs asking whether you deliver. For years the work of turning those conversations into paid orders was manual, scattered, and easy to drop. OnlyOrders [takes that flow and puts it on autopilot](https://onlyorders.tech): smart replies, live catalog checks, payment verification, and courier handoff, all from one inbox. This post is a clear-eyed, factual look at what the product actually is — the feature set and the pricing, as published on the product site.

## The one-line pitch

The company's own framing is worth quoting directly, because it captures the value proposition tightly:

> "OnlyOrders turns DMs and comments into confirmed orders — smart replies, live catalog checks, payment verification and courier handoff, all from one inbox."

The tagline is even shorter:**"Your orders, on autopilot."** The positioning is not about building a fancy storefront; it's about taking the conversations you already have and converting them into reliable commerce.

## What problem it's built around

For chat-first sellers, the pain points are familiar:

- Buying happens inside a conversation, not a checkout button.
- Across early in the week, the same customer might ask about stock on Instagram, confirm the price on WhatsApp, and ask about shipping on Messenger.
- Small teams can't staff three inboxes or answer at 11 p.m., so messages slip through.

OnlyOrders frames itself as the answer to exactly this — the "conversational commerce" layer for a chat-first store. Its hero line on the product page puts it plainly: everything a chat-first store needs, in one place.

## The headline features

The product site lists four core feature areas:

- **Instant replies** — AI answers customers 24/7.
- **One inbox** — every channel, zero tab chaos.
- **Chat to order** — receipts through to courier handoff.
- **Auto posts** — drops published on schedule.

Notice the shape here: it's not a chatbot bolted onto a store. It's a coordinated system where the AI handles the first touch, the order is captured inside the chat, and the fulfillment side (receipt, courier) is wired into the same loop.

## From first message to delivered order

The platform describes its workflow as a four-step journey, from "first message to delivered order":

1. **Messenger AI replies** — answers price, size, and stock questions in seconds, in your shop's tone, and hands off when a human is needed.
2. **Checkout inside the chat** — customers confirm the cart, address, and payment without leaving Messenger.
3. **One orders dashboard** — every chat order lands in a single board with status, notes, and totals.
4. **Courier tracking, auto-shared** — book the courier in one tap; tracking updates go back to the buyer's chat.

That arc — reply, checkout, dashboard, courier — is the entire commerce loop, and it stays inside the conversation the whole way.
